Biography

Albert Einstein was a German-born theoretical physicist who developed the theory of relativity and is widely considered one of the most influential scientists of all time.

Timeline

  • 1879: Born in Ulm, Germany.
  • 1905: Published four groundbreaking papers, including the theory of special relativity.
  • 1915: Developed the theory of general relativity.
  • 1921: Awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics for his explanation of the photoelectric effect.
  • 1933: Emigrated to the United States due to the rise of Nazism in Germany.
  • 1955: Died in Princeton, New Jersey, United States.

Quote

"Imagination is more important than knowledge."

- Albert Einstein
